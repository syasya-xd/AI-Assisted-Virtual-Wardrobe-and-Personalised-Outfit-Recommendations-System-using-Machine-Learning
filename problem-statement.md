# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Every user who manage their own clothing collections, particularly those who have multiple clothing items but experience difficulty on deciding what to wear and matching their existing clothes into suitable outfits based on every occasion.

## What exactly is going wrong?
User need to consider several factors simultaneously when selecting an outfit, including compatibility between clothing items, personal preferences, occasion, and colour suitability. Research on personalised fashion outfit generation shows that producing suitable outfits requires both personalisation and outfit compatibility, while personalised compatibility research has also considered individual characteristics such as skin colour and colour contrast.

## Why does it matter?
When recommendations do not adequately consider the individual user, the suggested outfits may be less relevant or less suitable to the user's preferences and characteristics. This makes outfit selection more difficult and limits the usefulness of generic fashion recommendation approaches. Research has demonstrated the importance of evaluating fashion recommendations from both personalisation and compatibility perspectives.
